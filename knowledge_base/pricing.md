# NexusCRM Pricing & Plans

## Overview

NexusCRM offers three pricing tiers designed for businesses of all sizes.
All plans include core CRM features. Add-ons are available for advanced AI capabilities.

---

## Starter Plan — $29/user/month

**Best for:** Small teams and startups (up to 5 users)

### Included Features
- Up to 1,000 leads
- Up to 500 customers
- Up to 50 active deals
- Email integration (Gmail, Outlook)
- Basic reporting dashboard
- Standard support (email, 48-hour response)
- Data export (CSV)
- Mobile app (iOS and Android)

### Limitations
- No AI agent features
- No API access
- No custom fields
- Single pipeline view

---

## Professional Plan — $79/user/month

**Best for:** Growing sales teams (up to 50 users)

### Included Features
- Unlimited leads and customers
- Unlimited deals and pipelines
- Full API access (REST + Webhooks)
- Advanced analytics and forecasting
- Custom fields and tags
- Email sequences and templates
- Priority support (email + chat, 4-hour response)
- Data import/export (CSV, Excel, JSON)
- Zapier and HubSpot integrations
- 2-factor authentication
- Audit logs (90-day retention)

### AI Add-on (Professional): +$20/user/month
- NexusCRM AI chat assistant
- Lead scoring (AI-powered)
- Email drafting assistant
- Basic RAG knowledge queries

---

## Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing (starts at $199/user/month)

**Best for:** Large organizations (50+ users) requiring advanced security and SLAs

### Included Features
- Everything in Professional
- Multi-tenant architecture (separate data silos per division)
- SAML/SSO integration (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace)
- Role-based access control with custom roles
- Dedicated infrastructure (private cloud or on-premise option)
- 99.9% uptime SLA with financial compensation
- Dedicated customer success manager
- Custom integrations (Salesforce data migration, SAP, NetSuite)
- Data residency options (EU, APAC, US)
- GDPR, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 compliance reports
- Advanced audit logs (unlimited retention)
- Priority phone support (24/7, 1-hour response)
- Annual contract with volume discounts

### AI Add-on (Enterprise): +$35/user/month
- Full NexusCRM AI multi-agent system
- Custom knowledge base (upload proprietary documents)
- LangSmith observability and tracing
- Evaluation pipeline for AI quality monitoring
- Custom agent personas
- Human-in-the-loop approval workflows
- Predictive pipeline analytics

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I switch plans mid-subscription?**
A: Yes. Upgrades are effective immediately (prorated billing). Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Is there a setup fee?**
A: No setup fees for Starter and Professional. Enterprise may include a one-time implementation fee depending on custom integration requirements.

**Q: What counts as a "user"?**
A: A user is anyone with a named login to NexusCRM. Shared/generic logins are not permitted under our terms of service.

**Q: Are API calls metered separately?**
A: For Professional, you get 100,000 API calls/month included. Enterprise includes unlimited API calls. Overages on Professional are $0.001/call.

**Q: Do you offer non-profit pricing?**
A: Yes — 40% discount for registered non-profits. Contact sales@nexuscrm.io with your 501(c)(3) documentation.
