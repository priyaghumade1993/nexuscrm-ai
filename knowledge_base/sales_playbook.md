# NexusCRM Sales Playbook

## Introduction

This playbook guides NexusCRM sales representatives through our proven sales methodology.
Follow these steps to maximize close rates and build long-term customer relationships.

---

## Sales Methodology: MEDDIC Framework

NexusCRM uses the **MEDDIC** qualification framework for enterprise deals.

### M — Metrics
- Quantify the business impact for the prospect
- Key questions: "How many leads does your team process monthly?" / "What is your current deal close rate?"
- Target: Identify at least 2 measurable pain points before advancing to demo

### E — Economic Buyer
- Identify the person with final purchase authority
- In SMBs: typically the CEO or VP Sales
- In Enterprise: CRO, CFO, or VP of Revenue Operations
- Rule: Never close a deal without at least one call with the economic buyer

### D — Decision Criteria
- Understand how the prospect will evaluate solutions
- Common criteria: integration with existing tools, data security, ease of use, AI capabilities, ROI
- Action: Send a customized ROI calculator after the discovery call

### D — Decision Process
- Map out their buying process and timeline
- Questions: "Who else is involved in the decision?" / "What does your approval process look like?"
- Typical enterprise timeline: 30–90 days from first demo to contract

### I — Identify Pain
- Uncover the core business problems driving their search
- Common pains: too many leads lost due to poor tracking, sales reps using spreadsheets, no pipeline visibility, manual email follow-ups
- Rule: Pain must be quantified in $ or % before a formal proposal

### C — Champion
- Find an internal advocate who benefits from your solution
- Champions are typically: SDR Managers, VP Sales, RevOps leads
- Nurture the champion with ROI data, case studies, and competitive comparison

## Lead Qualification — BANT

For inbound leads and SMB prospects, use **BANT**:

- **B**udget: Can they afford NexusCRM? (Starter $29, Pro $79, Enterprise custom)
- **A**uthority: Are they the decision-maker or a strong influencer?
- **N**eed: Do they have a clear CRM need vs. exploring casually?
- **T**imeline: Are they looking to implement within 90 days?

A lead is **Sales Qualified (SQL)** when it meets at least 3 of 4 BANT criteria.

## Follow-Up Cadence

### Post-Demo (Days 1–14)
- **Day 1**: Send meeting summary + ROI calculator (within 2 hours)
- **Day 3**: Share relevant case study or customer success story
- **Day 7**: Check in on internal discussions; offer to join a call with their team
- **Day 14**: Formal proposal if verbal interest confirmed

### Post-Proposal (Days 1–21)
- **Day 1**: Call to walk through proposal; address questions
- **Day 5**: Share competitive comparison doc
- **Day 10**: "Is there anything holding you back?" conversation
- **Day 21**: Create urgency with end-of-quarter pricing lock-in

### Stalled Deals (No response in 14+ days)
- Send a "break-up email": "I'll close this opportunity if we don't connect by [date]"
- This often re-engages lost prospects

## Objection Handling

### "It's too expensive"
- Reframe: "What's the cost of a lost deal due to poor lead tracking?"
- Offer: Annual plan saves 20%; ROI calculator shows payback in 60–90 days
- Escalate: Offer a call with our CFO for Enterprise pricing discussions

### "We're happy with our current CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot)"
- Acknowledge: "Salesforce is great for large enterprises — here's where we differ for your team size"
- Differentiate: NexusCRM AI is built-in; Salesforce Einstein costs extra $75/user/month
- Offer: Free migration service for up to 10,000 records

### "We need to involve IT/Legal"
- Welcome: Provide security questionnaire + SOC 2 report proactively
- Accelerate: Offer a 30-minute call with our CTO for IT teams
- Provide: DPA (Data Processing Agreement) template for legal review

### "We're not ready yet"
- Set a future date: "Can we reconnect in 3 months? I'll note a reminder."
- Keep warm: Monthly newsletter subscription
- Check: Is there a budget cycle they're waiting for?

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Salesforce
- **Advantage**: NexusCRM is 60% less expensive; built-in AI at no extra cost; 4x faster implementation
- **Disadvantage**: Smaller ecosystem; fewer native integrations
- **When we win**: Teams of 5–200 wanting AI-powered CRM without Salesforce's complexity

### vs. HubSpot
- **Advantage**: More powerful AI agent (multi-agent vs. single chatbot); better pipeline analytics
- **Disadvantage**: HubSpot has stronger marketing automation
- **When we win**: Sales-focused teams who need pipeline intelligence, not marketing campaigns

### vs. Pipedrive
- **Advantage**: AI capabilities, RAG knowledge base, multi-tenant for agencies
- **Disadvantage**: Pipedrive has a simpler UI for non-technical teams
- **When we win**: Technical teams who want API access + AI agent

## Discounting Policy

- **Standard discount authority**: Up to 10% (Sales Rep)
- **Manager approval required**: 10–20%
- **VP Sales approval required**: 20–30%
- **CEO approval required**: >30%
- **Never discount below**: 15% above our cost floor (Finance team has the table)
- Discounts require: minimum 12-month annual contract + payment upfront
