# NexusCRM Customer Support Policy

## Support Tiers

Support availability and response times depend on your subscription plan.

### Starter Plan Support
- **Channels**: Email only
- **Hours**: Business days 9AM–6PM IST (Mon–Fri)
- **Response time SLA**: 48 business hours for initial response
- **Scope**: Account setup, billing questions, basic feature guidance
- **Self-service**: Help center, video tutorials, community forum

### Professional Plan Support
- **Channels**: Email + live chat
- **Hours**: Extended — 8AM–10PM IST, Mon–Sat
- **Response time SLA**: 4 business hours for P1/P2; 24 hours for P3/P4
- **Scope**: All Starter issues + API troubleshooting, integration support, data migration assistance
- **Escalation**: Dedicated support engineer for complex issues

### Enterprise Plan Support
- **Channels**: Email + chat + phone + dedicated Slack channel
- **Hours**: 24/7/365
- **Response time SLA**: 1 hour for P1; 4 hours for P2; 8 hours for P3; 2 business days for P4
- **Scope**: Full technical support + proactive monitoring + quarterly business reviews
- **Dedicated CSM**: Named Customer Success Manager assigned at onboarding
- **Escalation**: Direct access to engineering team for critical production issues

## Priority Definitions

| Priority | Definition | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| P1 — Critical | Complete system outage; production data at risk | All users locked out; data loss detected |
| P2 — High | Core functionality broken; no workaround available | Cannot create leads; AI agent unresponsive |
| P3 — Medium | Feature degraded; workaround exists | Slow AI response; export formatting issue |
| P4 — Low | Minor issue; cosmetic or enhancement request | UI alignment; feature request |

## Refund & Cancellation Policy

### Monthly Plans
- Cancel anytime; access continues until end of current billing period
- No refunds for partial months used
- Data retained for 30 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted

### Annual Plans
- Cancel within 30 days of annual renewal for full refund
- After 30 days: no refund; access until end of annual term
- Early termination fee: 25% of remaining contract value

### Trial Cancellation
- Cancel at any time during the free trial with no charge
- No credit card required for 14-day trial

### Service Credits (SLA Violations)
If NexusCRM fails to meet uptime SLAs (99.9% monthly for Enterprise):
- 99.0–99.9%: 10% service credit for the affected month
- 95.0–99.0%: 25% service credit
- Below 95.0%: 50% service credit
Credits applied to next invoice; not redeemable as cash.

## Data Retention & Deletion

### Active Account Data
- CRM records: Retained indefinitely while subscription active
- Conversation history (MongoDB): 90 days default; Enterprise can configure longer
- Audit logs: 90 days (Professional); unlimited (Enterprise)
- Backups: 30-day rolling window

### After Cancellation
- CRM data: Retained for 30 days; customer can request export during this period
- Export format: JSON, CSV, or SQL dump (request via support ticket)
- After 30 days: All data permanently and irrecoverably deleted from all systems
- GDPR Right to Erasure: Honored immediately upon verified written request

## Training & Onboarding

### Starter
- Self-paced onboarding checklist in-app
- Help center articles and video tutorials

### Professional
- 1-hour live onboarding call with Customer Success team
- Admin training session (invite up to 5 people)
- 30-day check-in call

### Enterprise
- Dedicated onboarding project manager (6–8 week implementation plan)
- Role-specific training: Admin, Manager, Sales Rep tracks
- Custom AI knowledge base setup assistance
- Go-live support (dedicated engineer for launch day)
- Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) with ROI analysis
